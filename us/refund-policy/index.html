Return & Refund Policy

7-Day Replacement Guarantee • Fair & Transparent Returns

1. 7-Day Replacement Guarantee (DOA)

If any hardware component received is Dead on Arrival (DOA) or exhibits a manufacturing defect within 7 days of delivery, MAIJRUN Tech will replace the product immediately with an identical brand-new unit upon technical verification.

2. Eligibility Conditions for Return

  • The original product packaging, box, warranty stickers, manuals, and accessories must be intact.
  • Original purchase invoice / order receipt must be provided.
  • No signs of physical damage, broken ports, burned ICs, or customer mishandling.
  • For custom PC builds, our technical team will bench-test the specific malfunctioning component.

3. Refund Processing & Timelines

• If an identical replacement unit is unavailable, the customer is entitled to an immediate 100% full refund.

• bKash / Nagad: Refunds are processed directly back to the originating mobile wallet within 24 to 48 hours of verification.

• SSLCommerz (Credit / Debit Card): Card refunds are initiated within 3 business days; bank posting takes 5–10 business days depending on the issuing bank.

• Cash on Delivery (COD): For returned COD orders, refunds are dispatched via bKash or bank transfer upon warehouse product receipt.

How to Initiate a Return or RMA Claim

1. Call our RMA Helpdesk: +880 9612-MAIJRUN (10 AM - 8 PM)
2. Email pictures/video of the issue along with your Order ID to rma@maijrun.com
3. Our courier will arrange doorstep pickup or you may drop off at any authorized service hub.
